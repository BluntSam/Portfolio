About Blaint

Samuel E.H. | Blaint

Game Developer, Programmer, Designer, Artist

Hello! My name is Samuel. Though online I am called Blaint or BluntSam. I am heavily passionate about video games and how game mechanics are designed. I enjoy creating in-game systems, interactive experiences, and immersive stories. There’s folders of ideas I’ve had for games and written down that I’ve yet to do, but one day hope I can. I’ve previously worked well with others in teams and on my own creating personal projects.

I am currently in the Game Design & Simulation college program at Georgian College to learn professional level programming, game design, game asset creation, and group game development. This program is where I’ve helped my first group create Threadful, and is where I am helping my second group create Project Derelict. It is also where I met my friends, and we joined Ludum dare 59 together for fun.
